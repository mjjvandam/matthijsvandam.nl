# Expertisebeeld-instructies

Gebruik deze instructies voor nieuwe afbeeldingen bij expertise-tegels. Doel: herkenbare, rustige beelden die medisch kloppen zonder abstracte iconen, logo's of behandelclaims.

## Basisstijl

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: small expertise tile image for a Dutch professional healthcare website
Style: realistic editorial photography, calm Dutch outpatient or rehabilitation context, natural daylight, warm white, deep green, clay and subtle aqua accents. Human, professional, understated.
Composition: 4:3 or 1:1 crop-friendly image, clear single subject, enough quiet background, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no recognizable faces, no patient-identifying information.
Avoid: abstract shapes, icons, 3D symbols, AI glow, holograms, futuristic interfaces, dramatic pain poses, operation scenes, blood, needles, before/after imagery, weight-loss imagery, shaming visual language, stock-photo smiles, treatment guarantees.
```

## Onderwerpprompts

**Hallux valgus**
```text
Close editorial photo of a foot model and a neutral shoe-fit discussion on a consultation table, focused on forefoot alignment and bunion education. No bare identifiable patient foot in close-up.
```

**Hallux rigidus**
```text
Close editorial photo of a clinician pointing at the big-toe joint on a foot anatomy model beside a walking shoe with a stiff sole.
```

**Hamerteen en klauwteen**
```text
Close editorial photo of a foot anatomy model, toe spacer/protective padding and comfortable footwear on a calm consultation table.
```

**Metatarsalgie en Morton neuroom**
```text
Close editorial photo of an insole, metatarsal pad and foot model on a table, focused on pressure distribution under the forefoot.
```

**Enkelverzwikking**
```text
Editorial photo of an ankle model, elastic exercise band and balance board in a physiotherapy setting, focused on strength and balance recovery.
```

**Chronische enkelinstabiliteit**
```text
Editorial photo of a person from the knees down doing a controlled balance exercise with a physiotherapist nearby, no identifiable face.
```

**Artrose van de enkel**
```text
Editorial photo of an ankle anatomy model, supportive shoe and calm consultation notes, focused on stiffness and mobility rather than surgery.
```

**Platvoet bij volwassenen**
```text
Editorial photo of custom insoles, a foot posture model and walking shoes on a consultation table, focused on adult flatfoot support.
```

**Revisie na artrodese**
```text
Editorial photo of a foot-and-ankle bone model with neutral planning notes and CT-style images blurred in the background, no readable patient data.
```

**Artrose van de knie**
```text
Editorial photo of a knee anatomy model on a consultation table with a simple exercise plan, calm and non-alarming.
```

**Meniscus- en sportknieklachten**
```text
Editorial photo of a knee model, sports shoe and resistance band in a rehabilitation room, focused on sport-related knee assessment.
```

**Knieschijfklachten**
```text
Editorial photo of a knee model and physiotherapy exercise sheet with a resistance band, focused on patellofemoral mechanics and muscle function.
```

**Knieprothese**
```text
Editorial photo of a knee implant model or neutral knee anatomy model on a consultation table, with realistic preparation materials and no operating-room scene.
```

**Bewegen bij artrose**
```text
Editorial photo of walking shoes, a simple step plan and a knee model in a calm consultation or physiotherapy room.
```

**Obesitas en gewrichtsklachten**
```text
Respectful editorial photo of a supportive conversation about joint pain and movement: hands, exercise plan, knee model and chair-level composition; no scale, measuring tape or weight-loss framing.
```

**Voorbereiden op herstel**
```text
Editorial photo of a recovery preparation table with walking shoes, exercise band, nutrition note, sleep/rest checklist and knee or ankle model, no readable private data.
```

## Herbruikbare negatieve prompt

```text
No abstract blobs, no logo marks, no line icons, no generic medical cross, no futuristic AR/AI overlay, no unrealistic glowing interface, no American hospital stock style, no surgery, no blood, no identifiable patient, no medical advice text, no claim that treatment works.
```
