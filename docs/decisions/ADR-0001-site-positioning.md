# ADR-0001: Site Positioning

Status: Accepted

Decision owner: site owner / Matthijs

Owner acceptance: Accepted by Matthijs van Dam on 2026-07-03.

## Context

The current site positions drs. Matthijs van Dam as an orthopaedic surgeon in the Orthopedisch Centrum ETZ in Tilburg, with a professional focus on foot, ankle and knee surgery, sports injury, knee osteoarthritis, lifestyle-related joint complaints, research, education, regional collaboration and care development.

The site also presents Matthijs as an independent advisor for non-patient-bound care development on a project basis. This advice route is separate from patient care.

Current source basis:

- `AGENTS.md`
- `docs/site/CURRENT_SITE_STATE.md`
- `docs/site/SITE_POSITIONING.md`
- `docs/site/TARGET_AUDIENCES.md`
- `docs/site/INVARIANTS.md`

Uncertainties:

- Whether the combination of "orthopaedic surgeon and independent advisor" remains equally clear on all future pages: needs owner validation.
- Whether new project content remains sufficiently connected to orthopaedics, ETZ context, regional care and official care channels: needs owner validation.

## Decision

MatthijsVanDam.nl remains a calm professional expert hub, not a patient portal, appointment route or personal medical advice channel.

The site keeps three separated routes:

- patient-oriented general information;
- patient-related information for referrers and colleagues;
- non-patient-bound advice, education, projects and care development.

Mobility Clinic Tilburg, knee cartilage injury, cartilage transplantation, 3D planning and patient-specific instrumentation may remain visible in targeted knee, cartilage, foot/ankle or professional contexts. They must not become the homepage main narrative unless a new owner-approved decision is made.

## Consequences

Future content and navigation changes must identify the intended audience and route before publication.

Patient-facing pages must remain general and route personal questions to official care channels. Professional and consultancy pages may be personal and expert-led, but must stay connected to ETZ, vakgroep, region, research, education or concrete project experience.

SEO and AI visibility must support this positioning without expanding treatment scope or introducing commercial claims.

## What must not change without a new ADR

- The site must not become a patient portal, appointment route or channel for personal medical advice.
- The three audience routes must not be merged or blurred.
- Consultancy must not be presented as patient care.
- The homepage narrative must not be shifted toward consultancy, AI, Mobility Clinic, cartilage transplantation or another single theme without a new ADR.
- New or changed site positioning, target audience, audience-route separation, homepage narrative, expertise claim, service claim, treatment-scope claim or patient/professional/consultancy boundary requires an explicit ADR check.
- If such a structural positioning change is not already covered by an Accepted ADR, Codex must propose a new ADR and stop for owner validation before implementation.
